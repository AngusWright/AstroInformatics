About these demonstrations

These are controlled teaching simulations, not analyses of real survey data. Mathematical details and caveats are kept in each page’s explanation view. The galaxy-encounter page is a restricted statistical companion to the original Holmberg simulation prototype, not a self-consistent N-body calculation.
