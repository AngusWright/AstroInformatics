Restricted dynamics, real uncertainty propagation

Forty massless, collisionless test particles start on circular orbits in a unit-mass softened host potential. A half-mass perturber follows the prescribed straight path (−5 + vt, b). Its tidal force includes the indirect acceleration of the host-centred frame.

A kick–drift–kick leapfrog integrates 80 equal steps of 0.1, with softening length 0.3. The perturber is not deflected. Disc self-gravity, gas, dynamical friction and a live host are absent. The time step is fixed, not adaptively error-controlled; this is a teaching model, not a precision dynamical calculation.

The nominal calculation uses the arithmetic mean impact parameter and speed. Twelve independent pairs use b = b̄ exp(sZ − s²/2) and v = v̄ exp(sZ′ − s²/2), with s² = log(1 + f²), independent standard-Normal Z and Z′, and fractional input SD f. Each pair is drawn once, then held fixed throughout its trajectory. All members share the same initial disc. A nonlinear model’s prediction at the mean inputs need not equal its ensemble mean or median prediction.

The diagnostic counts particles at radius greater than 2.5. It is not an escaped or unbound mass fraction. The band is the full range of twelve predictions, not a credible or confidence interval. A small ensemble cannot establish tail probabilities. Ensemble spread describes this input distribution under this restricted model; it is not automatically a calibrated posterior or a probability for a real galaxy.

The original HolmbergSimulation source remains a separate prototype. This is an astronomical application of the lecture’s simulation principles, not an example reproduced from those notes.
